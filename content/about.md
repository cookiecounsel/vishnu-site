title: A lawyer who reads regulations against running software.

I am a lawyer based in Kerala, working in privacy law and commercial contracts. I hold a law degree and diplomas in international privacy laws , and have spent about four years as a privacy analyst.

I write about the part of the work I know best: how privacy regulation meets software, and how contracts carry its obligations. I start from what a system does, then map it to what each law requires.
