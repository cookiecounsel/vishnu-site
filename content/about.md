I am a lawyer based in Kozhikode, Kerala, working in privacy law and commercial contracts. I hold a law degree and diplomas in international privacy laws, and have spent about four years as a legal analyst, with a major focus on privacy law.

I write about the part of the work I know best: how privacy regulation meets software, and how contracts carry its obligations. I start from what a system does, then map it to what each law requires.

## What I work on

My daily work sits across the GDPR and UK GDPR, the CCPA and CPRA, India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 and the UK's PECR. I am also studying AI data governance, including the operation of the EU AI Act and the ISO/IEC 42001 management standard.

In practice that means drafting and maintaining data processing agreements, privacy policies, DPIAs and records of processing, assessing the risk of cross-border data transfers, and building vendor compliance frameworks for SaaS products, plugins and online stores.

## How I work

I work directly with engineers, product managers and leadership, so most of my output has to survive contact with code. A consent requirement is only useful if it becomes a banner that blocks the right scripts, a record that can be produced on request, and a setting a user can reverse as easily as they gave it.

I treat risk as a calibrated judgement, not a checklist. Where the law is settled, I say what it requires. Where it is not, I say so, set out the defensible positions, and recommend one with the reasons.

## Beyond law

I also write fiction: stories, serials and poems set in worlds of my own making. It has its own section and its own look, so the legal writing stays easy to find and the fiction stays easy to read.

## What you will find here

**Law.** Articles on consent, data processing agreements and cross-border data rules, written from the practitioner's side. Each states its date and is revised when the law or my view changes. They are general information, not legal advice.

**Fiction.** Short stories, poems and chapters of longer work, published as they are ready. All of it is fiction, and all rights are reserved.
